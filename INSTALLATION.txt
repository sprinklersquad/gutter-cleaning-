GODADDY GUTTER QUOTE CALCULATOR — VERSION 3

WHAT CHANGED
Customers see an initial cleaning quote with separate dollar lines for the house minimum, additional size, debris buildup, tree coverage, and selected downspout clearing. Included/zero-fee categories stay visible. They then compare one-time, annual, and spring/fall options, select a schedule, and send contact details for owner review. Maintenance prices start on future visits; annual totals exclude the initial cleaning. Four inline icons explain camera inspection, ground-based work, high-suction vacuum removal, and cleanup. No square-foot or linear-foot rates appear to customers.
Exceptions such as guards, gutters over 40 feet, difficult access, or more than six blocked downspouts receive a property-review path before any price or maintenance plan is offered.

PRICING POLICY TO REVIEW AS OWNER
The one displayed amount is a PACKAGE QUOTE based on the customer's answers, not an assertion that a house-size formula can measure real gutters exactly.
Customer-facing policy: the price stays the same when the answers match the property and requested work. If something differs, explain the difference and obtain approval before changing the price. Review requests do not book appointments or collect payment.
You are accepting estimation risk when accurate customer answers produce an unusual gutter footprint. Do not automatically increase the price just because the length model was wrong. Review your margins, tune the package formula with real jobs, or decline an unsupported scope during review. This package does not enforce contracts or handle payments.

OWNER SETTINGS — IN CONFIG NEAR THE SCRIPT TOP
Up to ordinary one-story height: $149 minimum / $1.25 internal foot.
Two stories or partial/split levels: $175 minimum / $1.75 internal foot.
Three stories: $250 minimum / $2.25 internal foot.
A walkout/slope increases a ranch to the middle band; a two-story home with that extra exposure uses the high band. A checked over-40-ft answer always routes to review without a price. Stories remain a rough proxy for working height and must be checked during review.
Internal gutter estimate = 3.5 * square root(above-ground area / stories) + 30 ft for typical extra runs, rounded up to 10 ft. This is a provisional, unvalidated estimating model. No range is displayed.
Base = greater of band minimum and internal feet times rate.
Visible debris charge: routine $0; heavy packed leaves/sludge $35; very heavy/plants $60. If the customer cannot see buildup, use cleaning history to estimate this ONE debris charge: within year $0; 1–2 years $25; over 2 years/never $50; unknown $25.
Tree coverage charge: light $0; moderate $10; heavy $25.
Debris buildup and tree coverage are now SEPARATE ADDITIVE fees. This intentionally changes version 2, which used only the larger fee. Cleaning history is not added on top of a known visible-buildup fee. The UI identifies the selected condition behind each line, and the resulting quote can be higher than in version 2.
Known accessible downspout blockage clearing: $35 per selected downspout, included in total. No known blockage/unknown count adds $0; unknown work is explicitly excluded pending inspection and a separate approved quote. Accessible outlet suction remains included.
Minimum-based quotes retain the exact $149 / $175 / $250 minimum plus applicable extras. Size-driven quotes round up to $5; the size-adjustment line includes any rounding.
No separate mileage surcharge is included in this version. Quoted packages assume residential service in El Paso County; collect ZIP before pricing and full address after acceptance, then verify county/service eligibility. ZIP alone does NOT validate county boundaries or driving distance. Travel within the county is included in package pricing; change the pricing policy and visible copy together if you want narrower service zones. Do not add an undisclosed mileage fee after acceptance.
No tax is separately calculated; review your own applicable tax treatment before using as an invoice. The tool produces a quote request, not a final invoice.

FUTURE MAINTENANCE OPTIONS
Annual: up to 5% off routine maintenance, one future visit per maintenance year.
Spring/fall: up to 10% off routine maintenance, two future visits per maintenance year.
The initial cleaning is full price with EVERY option. Initial buildup and today's selected downspout blockages are not recurring charges. Routine maintenance baseline is house base/size + tree coverage, subject to the same rounding policy. Maintenance discounts apply to that baseline, not to the initial cleaning. See PRICING-RESEARCH.txt for local and other published benchmarks.
Discounted prices round up to $5 and preserve the property's exact minimum; they cannot exceed standard maintenance. This can reduce or eliminate a discount. UI says “up to,” shows ACTUAL dollar savings from the maintenance baseline, and shows “House minimum applies” when there is no saving. Do not advertise a guaranteed 5% or 10% saving for every house.
Future maintenance-year total = future per-visit price times one or two. It excludes initial cleaning. No first-calendar-year total is calculated because no dates are selected; the initial visit is NOT counted as one of the future visits shown.
Routine buildup and the same accessible scope are assumed. New blockages or unusually heavy buildup require approval for extra work. Plans are requested for owner review and paid per completed visit. No automatic billing, appointment, multiyear term, lifetime rate lock, or cancellation penalty is implemented.

LEAD CONTENT AND SECURITY
Each request contains the initial dollar breakdown, initial price, chosen schedule, future per-visit price, future maintenance-year total, standard maintenance baseline and actual savings, plan terms, property ZIP/address, name, email, phone, notes, all input answers, version, and accepted-for-review status. One-time and custom-review requests do not claim a recurring price. Custom-review requests explicitly state no price was accepted. The owner must verify the inputs, scope, and price calculation before confirmation. Client-side prices and fields can be modified by a visitor; no server-side price verification is included.
Changing property answers clears the active quote and requires recalculation. Duplicate clicks are blocked while sending. Requests have a timeout and show failure with an email fallback. A timed-out request may still have reached the provider; customers are warned to check before resending.
The service endpoint can be replaced with your own HTTPS JSON-compatible endpoint via submissionUrl. Update response handling and privacy copy if changing providers. Never put private API keys in this public embed.
No customer payment, photo upload, scheduling calendar, browser storage, or analytics are included. No contact details leave the page until the visitor submits. The external processor and GoDaddy may have their own data handling.
