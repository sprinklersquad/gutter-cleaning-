RECOMMENDATION IMPLEMENTED
Annual maintenance: up to 5% off standard future maintenance.
Spring-and-fall maintenance: up to 10% off standard future maintenance.
These are proposed business rates informed by the offers above, not observed local average discount rates. Annual offers researched vary from no discount to 10%; twice-yearly examples commonly show 10%, with 15% available under different terms. A 5% / 10% approach gives a reason to return while retaining margin and emphasizing the twice-yearly option.

ECONOMICS AND SCOPE
The initial cleaning stays full price and addresses the current buildup and selected accessible downspout blockages. Future routine maintenance includes the house base/size price plus the property's tree-coverage charge. The initial debris surcharge and initial downspout blockage charges are not carried into every future visit.
Tree coverage remains relevant on recurring visits because the property keeps shedding leaves and needles. Recurring prices assume routine buildup at agreed intervals; unusually heavy new buildup or new blockages are separately assessed and require approval.
This is a scope change AND a maintenance discount. Do not label the entire difference between the initial cleaning and a maintenance visit as a plan discount. The calculator displays the standard future maintenance baseline and calculates actual savings from that baseline only.

MINIMUMS / ROUNDING
The existing property minimums ($149 / $175 / $250 by height band) remain floors for maintenance. Discounted maintenance rounds up to $5 and is capped at the standard maintenance price. Where the floor or rounding reduces the available discount, customers see the actual dollar savings; where there is no saving, they see “House minimum applies” instead of a savings claim.
Thus the labels are “up to 5%” and “up to 10%,” not promises that every property receives the full percentage.

ANNUAL TOTALS
Maintenance-year totals equal the discounted per-visit price times one or two future visits. They exclude the separately displayed initial cleaning. The initial visit is not automatically counted as one of those future maintenance visits. No first-calendar-year total is promised, since actual dates have not been selected.
Plans are requested for review, paid per completed visit, with no automatic billing or appointment booking. There is no implied multiyear contract or lifetime price lock. The owner confirms pricing, scope, and schedule before service. No specific scheduling, cancellation, or renewal terms beyond these are fabricated.

FIRST-CLEANING BREAKDOWN CHANGE
The customer's explicit visible buildup selection determines its fee: normal $0, heavy packed debris $35, very heavy/plants $60. If they cannot see the gutter, cleaning history estimates that fee: within year $0; 1–2 years $25; over two years $50; unknown $25.
Tree coverage is separately priced: light $0; moderate $10; heavy $25. It is added to the debris fee rather than taking only the larger of the two as older calculator versions did. This change is intentional to meet the requested separate debris and tree charges and can increase an initial quote. Cleaning history is NOT a third charge stacked on explicit buildup.
